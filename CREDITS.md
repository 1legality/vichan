The party hat is used in the birthday page banner and on photos for the site's birthday only. All other illustrations are restricted to page banners. Optimization uses ImageMagick 7.1.2-23: trim transparent margins, resize proportionally, retain 8-bit RGBA, strip metadata and choose the smaller lossless PNG compression result. The party, witch, Santa and jester hats retain their previously optimized maximum of 512 pixels. The fleur de lys and champagne glasses are limited to 256 pixels for their small banner display. The anti-cannabis sign retains its original 740 × 740 pixels: its ancillary PNG metadata is removed and its existing image data is recompressed with zlib without changing any pixels. No illustration is redrawn, recolored or generated.

Transparency was checked before and after optimization. All seven local PNGs contain fully transparent pixels (alpha 0), opaque pixels (alpha 255) and partially transparent edge pixels; no background was flattened. Original content bounds were 1374 × 1998 at +2+2 (party hat), 783 × 703 at +9+21 (witch), 800 × 556 at +0+0 (Santa), 798 × 390 at +1+1 (jester), 637 × 797 at +0+2 (fleur de lys), 800 × 643 at +0+0 (champagne glasses), and 740 × 740 at +0+0 (anti-cannabis sign). Together the local files use 397657 bytes, compared with 920351 bytes downloaded.
